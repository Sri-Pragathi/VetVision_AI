## Data Flow

1. **User Initiation**: Pet owner logs in, selects or creates a pet profile, and starts an assessment.
2. **Symptom Selection**: Pet parent selects observed signs; backend identifies relevant clinical categories.
3. **Adaptive Questioning**: `dynamic_question_service` evaluates answers in real-time and queries `question_bank` to present context-specific follow-ups.
4. **Visual Inspection**: User uploads photos; `cv_engine` checks image quality, framing, and anatomical features.
5. **Triage & Risk Calculation**: `risk_engine` combines symptoms, answers, and image findings to compute an evidence-weighted risk score (Low, Moderate, Urgent, Emergency).
6. **Report Generation**: `report_service` generates a comprehensive clinical report with veterinarian insights, triage status, and care guidelines.
